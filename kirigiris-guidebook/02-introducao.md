Welcome to Hope's Peak Academy.

From the project team

This guidebook is a *reference*, not *instructions on how to play "correctly"*.

Shinri Trial is first and foremost a roleplaying game: about characters, relationships, scenes and mutual roleplay, and only after that about crafting and stashes.

We gathered this information to make it easier for newcomers to get into the auto-games, not to turn them into a race for resources. Please use it with that caveat in mind.

— the Kirigiri's Press team

Sixteen talented students, one building with no easy way out. Investigate tangled cases, team up with other players, or outwit everyone and become the sole winner.

---

Shinri Trial is a social-deduction game with elements of crafting, looting and roleplay, where there is no prescribed plot. Every step, every word, every betrayal is the result of decisions made by living players. Here you are not a spectator but a full author of the story: maybe your character will become the key figure in an investigation or, on the contrary, the cold-blooded killer that others will try to expose.

---

This guidebook is an all-purpose guide. It collects everything you need: from the basic rules to the finer points that will come in handy for newcomers and veterans of the project alike. If you want to know how the world of mad mutual distrust works, how to investigate crimes and survive when every decision could be your last — read on. Your game has already begun.
