> ⚠ Maximum capsules: 8, calculated by the formula 55% of the number of survivors

## DICAS DE REPARO

- [Inventory] Sometimes it's better to carry all the parts with you during a repair so no one steals them. The alternative is to hide the parts in a container that is unlikely to be checked, or in a container closer to the repair site (for convenience), but at the very bottom of the container. From time to time, glance at the electrical panel, the supercomputer and the air purifier. This way you'll make sure the parts are in place and nothing has been stolen, and at the same time check whether someone has already brought the needed parts — this saves time searching for and crafting repair items.  (etapas: [0, 1, 2])
- [Access] Just in case, you can keep on you the staff badge, the classified facility blueprints and the staff-room keycard — they may be needed in the next repair stages.  (etapas: None)
- [Overload] When overloaded, drop the heavy parts on the floor, gather items from containers up to the yellow bar, and drag the dropped parts to the workbench by holding LMB and moving the mouse.  (etapas: None)
- [Pinning] Pin the repair recipe so the parts you need stay in front of you. It works just like pinning crafting recipes, so you no longer have to remember what is missing every time.  (etapas: None)
