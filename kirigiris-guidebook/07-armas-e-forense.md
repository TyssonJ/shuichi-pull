### Notas sobre venenos

- A Somnus grenade, sleeping pills and an electroshock let you stun or put a poisoned player to sleep and pause the poison’s timer — that buys time to craft the cure. Once the player comes round, the timer resumes from where it stopped.
- If you stun a player before the poison has taken effect (that is, before the «taken → onset» timer runs out), the poison’s timer keeps running while they are unconscious and can expire before they come round, neutralising the poison’s effect.
- A player cannot be poisoned while they are unconscious.
